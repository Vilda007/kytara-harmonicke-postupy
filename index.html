What's inside

🎯 Circle of fifths, per page

The key is highlighted on the circle: I green, V red, IV orange, vi blue; arrow shows the V→I dominant move.

🎸 Real fingering

Diatonic chords with fretboard shapes — barre start-fret marked, vii° with a practical V7 substitute.

🎶 The classics

I–IV–V, pop four-chord, doo-wop, ii–V–I and 12-bar blues — rewritten in every key.

🐍 Generated

Python data tables → HTML → PDF. Fix a typo, rerun, re-render. Fully reproducible.

  • 3-page theory from zero: semitones & scale building · chord function & the fifth-circle (incl. minor roles) · practice — reading diagrams, barre, capo, transposition.
  • The "easiest path" tip: capo plus friendly keys instead of fighting a barre you don't need yet.
Rule of thumb: V is one step right, IV one step left, vi sits under the tonic.
